# TOQAEE — Build Plan

## 1. Storefront foundation — DONE
Next.js App Router project, brand system, AI-generated product photography (served from project image URLs), marketplace-shaped domain types (`src/lib/types.ts`), stub catalogue (`src/lib/fixtures.ts`) behind a read layer (`src/lib/catalog.ts`).
Live: `/`, `/products`, `/products/[slug]`, `/products/category/[category]`, `/cart` (browser-persisted, grouped by seller, coupon TOQAEE10), `/track-order` (7-step workflow), `/admin/inventory` (source type, status, stock badge, low-stock rules — client-side only).

## 2. Database — FOUNDATION IMPLEMENTED
PostgreSQL schema, migrations, fixture seeds and storage repositories are in place. Tables include sellers (kind platform/partner/marketplace, commission_rate), users (role customer/admin/seller, email or Google identity), addresses, categories, products (seller_id, source_type, status, stock and payment-method choices), wishlists, orders, payments, seller_orders (per-seller sub-orders), order_items, order_events, coupons, banners, content pages and business settings. Admin catalogue/inventory management, customer profiles/addresses/wishlist/orders, payment verification, and public storefront reads use database repositories. Public product, category, seller, banner, coupon, content and business-setting changes are read dynamically so admin updates appear on the next storefront request. Configure `DATABASE_URL`, then run `npm run db:migrate` and `npm run db:seed`. Payouts, withdrawals and commission ledger are not implemented.

## 3. Auth & customer dashboard — EMAIL + GOOGLE CUSTOMER AUTH
`/login`, `/register`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`, `/profile`, `/profile/orders`, `/profile/order/[id]`, `/profile/wishlist`, `/profile/addresses`, and `/profile/edit-profile` exist. Customer accounts use signed-cookie sessions and database-backed records, with email/password and Google OIDC registration/login. Password recovery sends expiring, single-use links by email; customer order details load the owner's saved items, payment, delivery address, and tracking events from PostgreSQL. Super admins can edit, disable, and re-enable customer accounts; status changes invalidate existing customer sessions, including Google sign-ins. Configure a production `CUSTOMER_AUTH_SECRET`, Resend email settings, and Google OAuth credentials before enabling the related features in deployment. Admin sign-in at `/admin/login` uses `ADMIN_EMAIL`, `ADMIN_PASSWORD`, and `ADMIN_AUTH_SECRET` for the super admin; order-admin accounts are created and managed by the super admin and stored as password hashes in PostgreSQL. Signed sessions carry roles, and disabling/resetting an order-admin account invalidates its sessions.

## 4. Checkout & payments — GUEST COD + MOCK BKASH/NAGAD
`/checkout` supports guests and signed-in customers without requiring an account. It validates delivery and optional billing addresses, places orders transactionally, reserves stock, stores order snapshots/totals/notes, and creates seller sub-orders with commission snapshots. Product payment methods are enforced both at checkout and on the server. Guest COD works without login; signed-in customers may choose COD, bKash or Nagad when every cart item permits it and a valid manual payment number is configured. Payment records support pending/paid/failed/refunded states, with role-limited admin verification. bKash and Nagad currently use explicit mock providers and remain pending until admin verification; live gateway APIs, callbacks/webhooks, actual refund transfers, and reconciliation remain TODO. New orders are available through mobile/order-number tracking.

## 5. Admin console — CATALOGUE + PAYMENT MANAGEMENT
Products list/add/edit/delete, inventory, categories, partner-shop profiles and commission settings, dashboard metrics, customer search/profile/order history/account controls, banners/offers, content pages, coupons, business settings, notice text, manual bKash/Nagad numbers, order monitoring/status progression/cancellation, payment verification and order-admin account management use the database storage layer. Pausing a partner shop hides its products from browsing and checkout without deleting product or order history. Public storefront pages reflect catalogue, marketing, content and contact changes; notices appear in the storefront header and configured wallet numbers appear at checkout. Super admins manage all features; order admins can advance delivery status, cancel before delivery with stock restoration, verify payments, and record manual refunds.
